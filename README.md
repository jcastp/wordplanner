# Word count planner

This little application intends to make a plan for writing words.

To write, you need three variables:
- time in days to write
- total amount of words
- daily words

You need any two of these variables to fix the third one. So, there are three methods to do so:

1. Given the total time you want to write and the daily amount of words, it will calculate the total amount of words.
2. Given the total time you want to write and the total amount of words, it will calculate the daily amount of words you need to reach your goal.
3. Given the total amount of words you want to write and the daily amount you plan to write, it will calculate the time you need to complete your work.
